# Effect of light and nutrients on interspecific interactions between submerged macrophytes: implications for restoration of multispecies aquatic vegetation in eutrophic lakes

## Fuente
- Chat title: Revisión Criterios Exclusión Plantas Acuáticas
- Documento: Effect of light and nutrients on interspecific interactions between submerged macrophytes.pdf
- Insight title: Effect of light and nutrients on interspecific interactions between submerged macrophytes: implications for restoration of multispecies aquatic vegetation in eutrophic lakes

## Sintesis
- Estudio experimental en invernadero sobre restauracion multiespecie de macrofitas sumergidas.
- Compara interacciones entre Vallisneria natans y Potamogeton crispus bajo dos niveles de luz y dos de nutrientes.
- La recomendacion aplicada principal es asegurar suficiente luz y preferir plantacion en parches para favorecer coexistencia.
